# Industries Served

## Primary Industries

### Aerospace

IPS brands support commercial aerospace, business jets, aircraft interiors, propulsion, sealing, containment, fire protection, EMI protection, and composite-related applications.

### Defense

Defense use cases include aerospace defense, military advanced materials, radar cross-section reduction, EMI/RFI shielding, fire protection, thermal protection, naval hose and bellows, and mission-critical elastomeric components.

### Space

Rubbercraft public materials reference space ablatives and space-market support. This should be framed as a specialized high-performance segment rather than a broad consumer-facing claim.

### Medical And Biomedical

IRP Medical focuses on medical device elastomeric components, including liquid silicone and rubber molded engineered components. IPS sources also reference biomedical solutions.

### Semiconductor

IPS sources mention semiconductor markets across the platform. NES and RMB are especially relevant through elastomeric technologies, high-purity vessels, corrosion-resistant linings, and demanding process environments.

### Energy, Power, Oil And Gas

NES references energy and power. RMB references oil and gas and corrosion-resistant linings. IPS should present this as high-performance industrial infrastructure and process protection.

### Advanced Industrial And Commercial

The portfolio supports robotics, commercial electronics, industrial, printing/converting roller applications, and other specialty industrial markets.
