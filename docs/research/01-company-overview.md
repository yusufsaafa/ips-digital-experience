# IPS Company Overview

## Snapshot

Integrated Polymer Solutions (IPS) positions itself as a global leader in advanced materials. Its public site presents IPS as a portfolio of specialized businesses that solve demanding material, sealing, shielding, thermal, containment, and polymer manufacturing challenges.

IPS serves high-value markets where component failure is expensive, dangerous, or mission-limiting. Public sources consistently describe IPS around aerospace, defense, medical, semiconductor, energy, industrial, and specialty applications.

## Positioning Themes

- Advanced materials platform, not a single-product manufacturer.
- Engineering-led partner for critical applications.
- Portfolio of specialist brands with deep category credibility.
- Ability to serve OEM, aftermarket, defense, medical device, semiconductor, energy, and industrial customers.
- Emphasis on tight tolerances, qualification, certifications, and mission-critical reliability.

## Public Brand Message

IPS's official homepage headline states: "We Are IPS: The Global Leader in Advanced Materials" and the supporting line "Shaping the Future." The site prompts visitors to select an IPS business to explore deeper capabilities.

## Ownership And Corporate Context

Arcline Investment Management acquired IPS in 2019. Earlier private-equity sources describe IPS as headquartered in Long Beach, California and focused on highly engineered elastomeric components and tools for aerospace, military, biopharma, and specialty industrial end markets.
