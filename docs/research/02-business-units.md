# IPS Business Units

IPS is best understood as a portfolio of specialized manufacturing brands. The official IPS site invites visitors to choose a business and "dive deeper into their capabilities."

## Business Unit Map

| Business | Primary Focus | Markets And Signals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ABBA Roller | New and reconditioned high-precision rubber and chrome rollers | Precision roller applications; custom rubber chemistry; in-house compounding and laboratory testing |
| AkroFire | Fire and thermal management solutions | Aerospace OEM, aftermarket, and defense; composite firewall, high-temperature insulation, fireproof composite products |
| Icon Aerospace Technology | Bespoke sealing and rubber hose solutions | Aerospace and defense; sealing, containment, propulsion, protection systems |
| IRP Medical | Critical-to-function elastomeric solutions | Medical device components; liquid silicone and rubber molding; ISO 13485 and FDA registration noted by IPS |
| MAST Technologies | RF, microwave, and EMI-absorbing materials | Defense and commercial applications; radar cross-section reduction, thermal protection, electrical conductivity |
| Northern Engineering Sheffield (NES) | Advanced elastomeric technologies | Semiconductor, medical, energy and power, advanced industrial; scalable bespoke-to-production manufacturing |
| Oldham Seals | Elastomeric and polymer products for naval applications | Thermal protection, fire resistance, pressure containment; Jetpac fire-resistant hoses and bellows |
| RMB Products | Rotational molding, lining, and additive manufacturing | Aerospace, defense, semiconductor, oil and gas; high-purity vessels, corrosion-resistant linings, flight hardware |
| Rubbercraft | Full-capability polymer solutions | Commercial aerospace, defense, space, industrial; seals, ablatives, EMI solutions, composite fabrication enabling |
| Spira Manufacturing | EMI/RFI shielding | Aerospace, defense, and commercial environments; gaskets, shielded honeycomb filters, custom shielding |
| Swift Textile Metalizing (STM) | Metalized conductive fabrics | Aerospace, defense, and commercial applications; metalized cloth, custom fabrics, alloys, coatings |

## Digital Implication

The website should let visitors enter through three doors:

- By market: "I work in aerospace, defense, medical, semiconductor, energy..."
- By challenge: "I need sealing, shielding, thermal protection, corrosion protection..."
- By business: "I already know Rubbercraft, Spira, MAST, IRP Medical..."
