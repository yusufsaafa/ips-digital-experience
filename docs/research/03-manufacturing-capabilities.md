# Manufacturing Capabilities

## Capability Clusters

### Elastomeric Engineering And Molding

- Precision-molded elastomeric components.
- Liquid silicone rubber and rubber molded medical components.
- Fireproof and high-temperature elastomeric products.
- Encapsulated O-rings, seals, gaskets, hoses, bellows, and inflatable seals.
- Custom rubber chemistry, in-house compounding, and batch testing in applicable business units.

### Sealing, Containment, And Hose Systems

- Aerospace and defense sealing.
- Rubber hose solutions.
- Naval hose and bellow systems for fire resistance, pressure containment, and thermal protection.
- Bespoke products for sealing, containment, propulsion, and protection systems.

### EMI, RFI, RF, Microwave, And Signature Materials

- EMI/RFI shielding gaskets.
- Shielded honeycomb filters.
- Conductive metalized fabrics.
- RF, microwave, and EMI-absorbing materials.
- Materials for radar cross-section reduction, thermal protection, and electrical conductivity.

### Composite And Thermal Protection

- Fire seals.
- Composite firewall products.
- High-temperature insulation systems.
- Space ablatives.
- Composite fabrication enabling products.

### Rotational Molding, Lining, And Additive Manufacturing

- Rotational molding and lining.
- High-purity vessels.
- Corrosion-resistant linings.
- Seamless liners for pipelines and equipment.
- Additive manufacturing for advanced flight hardware.

### Tooling And Scale-Up

- Engineering and tooling support from prototype through production.
- Portfolio-level custom tooling depth. Arcline's acquisition announcement cited more than 6,000 custom tools used to produce more than 12,000 components.
- Production range from bespoke/single products to full-rate production, depending on business unit.

## Certifications And Compliance Signals

Public IPS/business-unit materials reference certifications and registrations including AS9100, ISO 9001, ISO 13485, FDA registration, ITAR, and compliance signals around REACH, RoHS, DFAR, FCC, EC, HIRF, and TEMPEST for applicable shielding products. These should be verified at the business-unit level before publication in customer-facing copy.
