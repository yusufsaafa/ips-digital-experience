# Core Technologies

## Engineering Domains

### Advanced Elastomers

The foundational IPS technology family is engineered elastomers: seals, gaskets, O-rings, molded components, hoses, bellows, fireproof seals, inflatable seals, and rubber-to-material bonded assemblies.

### Silicone And Rubber Molding

IRP Medical and related IPS capabilities point to liquid silicone rubber, rubber molding, tooling, and scale-up from prototype to volume production.

### High-Temperature And Fire-Resistant Materials

AkroFire, Oldham, Rubbercraft, and Icon support thermal/fire applications, including fire seals, composite firewall products, high-temperature insulation, fire-resistant hoses and bellows, and fireproof composite products.

### EMI/RFI Shielding And Conductive Materials

Spira, Swift Textile Metalizing, Rubbercraft, and MAST cover a broad shielding stack: EMI/RFI shielding gaskets, shielded honeycomb filters, conductive fabrics, metalized cloth, conductive elastomeric products, and RF/microwave absorbing materials.

### Signature And Absorptive Materials

MAST's public positioning centers on RF, microwave, and EMI-absorbing materials, with applications including radar cross-section reduction, thermal protection, and electrical conductivity.

### Fluoropolymer, Lining, And Corrosion Protection

RMB references Teflon PFA, high-temperature thermoplastics, high-purity vessels, corrosion-resistant linings, rotational lining, and seamless liners for pipelines and equipment.

### Composite Fabrication Support

Rubbercraft references composite fabrication enabling products. IPS sources also describe tooling for composite manufacturing and composite parts/enablers.

## Technology Story

IPS's strongest story is not "we make polymers." It is "we engineer advanced material systems for harsh environments where sealing, shielding, thermal control, pressure containment, corrosion resistance, or exacting tolerances decide whether a platform performs."
