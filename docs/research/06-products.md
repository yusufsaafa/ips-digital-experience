## Product Content Strategy

Product pages should avoid becoming a flat catalog. They should connect each product family to:

- Environment: heat, pressure, fuel, corrosion, EMI/RFI, clean process, dynamic motion.
- Application: aircraft, naval platform, semiconductor process, medical device, electronics enclosure.
- Proof: certifications, materials, tolerances, test methods, quality systems, case examples.
- Next action: consult engineering, select a business, request quote, upload drawing/specification.
