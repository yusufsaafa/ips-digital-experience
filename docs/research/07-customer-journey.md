# Customer Journey

This is a recommended journey model for the IPS Digital Experience. It is inferred from IPS's business model, public portfolio structure, and B2B advanced-manufacturing buying behavior.

## Journey Stages

### 1. Problem Recognition

Visitor arrives with a high-stakes need:

- Existing part is failing in heat, pressure, chemical, EMI/RFI, cleanroom, or dynamic-load conditions.
- A new platform requires qualified sealing, shielding, thermal, or molded material components.
- Procurement needs an approved supplier with technical depth and compliance credibility.

Primary content need: immediate confirmation that IPS solves hard material problems in the visitor's market.

### 2. Orientation

Visitor asks:

- Is IPS relevant to my industry?
- Which business unit should I talk to?
- Does IPS make products or custom engineered solutions?
- Does IPS have the certifications and scale I need?

Primary content need: industry entry points, challenge-based entry points, and a clear portfolio map.

### 3. Capability Matching

Visitor narrows from market to capability:

- Sealing.
- EMI/RFI shielding.
- Fire and thermal protection.
- Medical elastomeric molding.
- Rotational lining or high-purity vessels.
- Additive manufacturing.
- Rollers.

Primary content need: capability pages with product families, materials, applicable brands, and proof.

### 4. Proof And Risk Reduction

Visitor evaluates credibility:

- Certifications.
- Quality systems.
- Past market experience.
- Facilities and scale.
- Technical support.
- Materials expertise.
- Compliance and defense readiness.

Primary content need: proof modules, certifications, case studies, standards, testing language, and engineering process details.

### 5. Contact And Qualification

Visitor is ready to speak with engineering, sales, or a business unit.

Primary content need: a contact path that captures industry, application, target product/capability, drawings/specs, program timing, compliance needs, and preferred IPS business if known.

### 6. Engineering Collaboration

Post-contact journey likely includes NDA, drawing/spec review, material selection, prototype/tooling, qualification, production planning, and supply-chain coordination.

Primary content need: set expectations before contact so serious buyers understand IPS can support a technical development path.

## Recommended CTAs

- Talk to an engineer.
- Find the right IPS business.
- Request a quote.
- Submit a drawing or specification.
- Explore capabilities.
- View certifications.

## Friction To Remove

- Portfolio confusion: make it easy to understand which brand does what.
- Capability ambiguity: distinguish products, custom engineering, and manufacturing services.
- Contact uncertainty: route inquiries without forcing the visitor to know the correct business unit.
- Proof gap: surface certifications and market-specific credibility before the contact form.
