# User Personas

These personas are inferred from IPS's public markets, product categories, and B2B advanced-manufacturing buying journey.

## Aerospace Program Engineer

### Context

Designs or supports aircraft systems where sealing, fire protection, weight, reliability, and qualification matter.

### Needs

- Confirm IPS understands aerospace operating environments.
- Find relevant sealing, fire, hose, shielding, or elastomeric capability quickly.
- See proof of quality, materials expertise, and production readiness.
- Speak with an engineer, not a generic sales inbox.

### Likely Entry Point

Industry page for aerospace, capability page for sealing/fire protection, or known business such as Rubbercraft, Icon, AkroFire, Spira, or MAST.

## Defense Systems Buyer

### Context

Evaluates suppliers for mission-critical defense platforms, often with compliance, controlled technology, and reliability constraints.

### Needs

- Validate defense relevance and regulated-program readiness.
- Identify EMI/RFI, RF absorbing, thermal, pressure, sealing, or naval product fit.
- Understand which IPS business owns the capability.
- Route inquiry securely and efficiently.

### Likely Entry Point

Defense industry page, EMI/RFI shielding capability, RF/microwave absorbing materials, or Oldham/MAST/Spira/STM business pages.

## Medical Device Product Lead

### Context

Owns product development or supplier selection for medical device elastomeric components.

### Needs

- Find medical-specific elastomeric and liquid silicone rubber capabilities.
- Confirm regulated manufacturing signals such as ISO 13485 and FDA registration where applicable.
- Understand prototype-to-volume path.
- Contact a team that can review drawings and critical-to-function requirements.

### Likely Entry Point

Medical devices industry page or IRP Medical business page.

## Semiconductor Process Engineer

### Context

Works on high-purity, chemical, pressure, or process-equipment challenges.

### Needs

- Confirm high-purity and corrosion-resistance relevance.
- Find elastomeric, lining, vessel, and sealing capabilities.
- Understand materials compatibility and manufacturing scale.
- Reach a technical contact for application review.

### Likely Entry Point

Semiconductor industry page, corrosion protection, high-purity vessels, NES, or RMB Products.

## Procurement And Supplier Quality Manager

### Context

Compares qualified suppliers after engineering has identified a need.

### Needs

- Certifications and compliance signals.
- Markets served and customer credibility.
- Facility/manufacturing confidence.
- Clear RFQ path and business-unit routing.

### Likely Entry Point

About, certifications, resources, business unit, or contact/RFQ page.

## Executive Or Strategic Partner

### Context

Evaluates IPS as a platform, partner, acquisition ecosystem, or supplier network.

### Needs

- Understand IPS's scale and portfolio logic.
- See market coverage and differentiated technologies.
- Recognize the combined value of specialist businesses.
- Contact IPS corporate leadership or business development.

### Likely Entry Point

Home page, About IPS, Businesses, or company overview content.
