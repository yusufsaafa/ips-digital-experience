# Visitor Goals

## Primary Goals

- Determine whether IPS solves the visitor's specific material, sealing, shielding, thermal, fire, medical, high-purity, corrosion, or advanced-manufacturing challenge.
- Find the right IPS business without already knowing the portfolio.
- Validate technical credibility before contacting IPS.
- Understand product families and custom-engineering capabilities.
- Request a quote or engineering consultation.
- Submit a drawing, specification, or application description.

## Secondary Goals

- Compare IPS against known competitors.
- Confirm market experience in aerospace, defense, medical, semiconductor, energy, space, or industrial applications.
- Check certification and compliance signals.
- Learn whether IPS supports prototype, tooling, qualification, and production scale.
- Visit a specific business-unit website.
- Explore career, news, corporate, or partner information.

## Visitor Questions The Website Must Answer

- Is IPS one company, many brands, or both?
- Which business unit handles my problem?
- Do I start by industry, product, or capability?
- Does IPS make standard products, custom components, or both?
- Can IPS handle regulated or mission-critical programs?
- How do I get a technical conversation started?
- What information should I prepare before contacting IPS?

## Conversion Goals

- Contact engineering.
- Request quote.
- Submit drawing/specification.
- Select or discover relevant business unit.
- Download or view certification/resource content.
- Continue to a business-unit website with high intent.

## Content Requirements By Goal

| Visitor Goal | Required Content |
| --- | --- |
| Find relevance | Industry and capability entry points |
| Find right business | Portfolio map and routing prompts |
| Validate credibility | Certifications, facilities, quality systems, application examples |
| Understand offering | Product families, materials, manufacturing capabilities |
| Start project | Contact form with technical fields and upload support |
| Reduce supplier risk | Compliance, quality, market history, engineering process |
