# Story Flow From Landing Page To Contact

This story flow describes the proposed visitor path for a technical B2B buyer moving from initial awareness to qualified contact. It is a strategic recommendation, not a verified description of the current IPS website.

## Story Model

The experience should follow:

1. Problem.
2. Solution capability.
3. Proof.
4. Relevant IPS specialists.
5. Contact.

The visitor should not have to understand IPS's corporate structure before recognizing that IPS can help solve a critical technical problem.

## 1. Landing: Name The Critical Problem

The visitor immediately sees that IPS helps solve high-stakes material and component problems in demanding environments.

Content beats:

- Concrete problem-oriented promise.
- Critical problem examples: mission-critical sealing, EMI/RFI shielding, fire and thermal protection, corrosion and chemical resistance, high-purity processing, harsh-environment material performance.
- Primary CTA: Talk to an engineer.
- Secondary CTA: Explore problems we solve.

Story purpose:

- Meet the visitor at the moment of need.
- Replace vague corporate positioning with recognizable technical stakes.

## 2. Problem Selection: Let The Visitor Choose Their Failure Mode

The visitor chooses the problem language that matches their situation.

Pathways:

- Mission-critical sealing.
- EMI/RFI shielding.
- Fire and thermal protection.
- Corrosion and chemical resistance.
- High-purity processing.
- Harsh-environment material performance.

Story purpose:

- Let visitors self-identify without needing to know the correct product, capability, or IPS specialist.

## 3. Industry Context: Confirm The Operating Environment

The experience shows where the problem appears and why the operating environment matters.

Examples:

- Aerospace may involve heat, fuel exposure, weight, dynamic loads, sealing reliability, EMI, and qualification needs.
- Defense may involve EMI/RFI, radar signature, fire, thermal, naval pressure containment, controlled programs, and reliability.
- Medical devices may involve critical-to-function elastomeric components, molding quality, scale-up, and regulated-manufacturing expectations.
- Semiconductor applications may involve high purity, chemical resistance, corrosion protection, and precision.

Story purpose:

- Show the visitor that IPS understands both the problem and the market context around it.

## 4. Capability Match: Connect Problem To Solution

The page maps the selected problem to relevant IPS solution capabilities.

Content beats:

- Related capabilities.
- Materials and manufacturing technologies.
- Product families.
- Engineering considerations.
- Relevant industries.

Story purpose:

- Turn problem recognition into solution confidence.
- Help technical buyers see a plausible path from requirement to engineered component.

## 5. Engineering Process: Show How IPS Reduces Execution Risk

The visitor sees a clear proposed collaboration journey.

Process:

- Discover: clarify application, environment, failure mode, requirements, constraints, and program stage.
- Engineer: select materials, refine design, review manufacturability, and define the technical approach.
- Prototype: develop samples, tooling approaches, or early components where appropriate.
- Validate / Qualify: test, document, and support customer qualification requirements.
- Manufacture: transition to repeatable production through the relevant IPS specialist.
- Support: sustain production, aftermarket/MRO needs, lifecycle changes, and future iterations.

Story purpose:

- Build confidence for technical buyers who need more than a product list.
- Show that IPS can support the journey from unresolved problem to manufactured solution.
- Improve inquiry quality by teaching visitors what information IPS may need.

## 6. Proof: Lower Perceived Supplier Risk

The visitor sees evidence that IPS can handle high-stakes work.

Proof beats:

- Business-unit experience.
- Certifications and registrations, verified by business unit before publication.
- Engineering and tooling support where validated.
- Prototype-to-production path.
- Harsh-environment product examples.
- Defense or regulated-market readiness where applicable and verified.

Story purpose:

- Help engineering, procurement, and supplier-quality audiences justify IPS as a credible supplier or development partner.

## 7. IPS Specialist Network: Route To Depth Without Making Structure The Starting Point

The visitor is introduced to the portfolio as "one IPS platform, multiple specialized engineering companies."

Content beats:

- Specialist companies mapped to the selected problem, capability, industry, or product family.
- Concise specialist descriptions.
- Links to specialist websites where useful.
- CTA: Find the right IPS specialist.

Story purpose:

- Preserve the credibility of known brands while avoiding early portfolio confusion.
- Let visitors access specialist depth without needing to understand IPS's corporate structure first.

## 8. Contact: Capture Technical Intent

The contact experience should qualify the request without feeling like an obstacle.

CTA choices:

- Talk to an engineer.
- Request a quote.
- Submit a drawing or specification.
- Find the right IPS specialist.

Recommended fields:

- Name, company, email, phone.
- Industry.
- Problem or application challenge.
- Product or capability interest.
- Program phase: concept, prototype, qualification, production, MRO/aftermarket.
- Required certification/compliance.
- Preferred IPS specialist, if known.
- Upload drawing/specification.
- Timeline and quantity range.

Story purpose:

- Convert the visitor's technical problem into a routeable IPS opportunity.
- Allow IPS to triage inquiries by problem, industry, capability, and specialist fit.

## 9. Confirmation: Set Expectations

After submission, the visitor should understand what happens next.

Content beats:

- Confirmation that the inquiry was received.
- Expected response window, if verified by IPS.
- Summary of submitted details.
- Optional links to relevant specialist, problem, or capability pages.

Story purpose:

- Maintain confidence after conversion and signal operational discipline.

## Claims And Verification Rule

All claims about certifications, performance, facilities, market leadership, customer examples, and response timelines should be reviewed with IPS stakeholders before publication. This story flow is a proposal for experience strategy.
